Frequently Asked Questions

What is nurse delegation? expand_more

Nurse delegation is a process in Washington State that allows a registered nurse (RN) to delegate specific nursing tasks to qualified nursing assistants or home care aides for clients in certain settings, ensuring they receive safe and appropriate care.

Who is eligible for these services? expand_more

Eligibility is determined based on the client's stability, the specific task to be delegated, and the competency of the caregiver. Our RNs conduct a thorough assessment to ensure all state requirements are met before delegation occurs.

How do I start the process? expand_more

To begin, simply fill out the contact form on this page or call our office directly. We will schedule an initial consultation to discuss your needs and determine the best course of action for providing care.
